## 4. Visual Identity

### 4.1 Logo

The official CTRS logo is a **circular crest** featuring:
- **Shape:** Double-ring circle with text running around the outer ring
- **Outer Text (top arc):** "CHRIST THE REDEEMER'S SCHOOLS"
- **Outer Text (bottom ribbon):** "EDUCATION FOR GOD'S GLORY."
- **Inner Shield:** Divided into four quadrants with a cross motif, containing icons representing: a graduation mortarboard, a dove/bird, a microscope, and an open book
- **Abbreviation Text inside shield:** "C.T.R.S"
- **Star accents** on the left and right of the inner ring
- **Colour palette of the logo:** Forest green (#1A5C2A or similar), white, and dark green outline

> **Usage Note:** The CTRS logo appears prominently on student blazer breast pockets, school signage, and the school bus. Always maintain clear space around the logo equal to the height of the "C" in CTRS.

### 4.2 Brand Colour Palette

The following colours are drawn directly from the school's physical identity — uniforms, building, signage and interiors.

| Colour Name | Role | Hex Reference | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| Forest Green | Primary / Brand | `#1B5E3B` | Logo, blazers, signage board |
| Emerald Green | Secondary / Accent | `#2E8B57` | Signage, outdoor banners |
| Warm Orange / Amber | Accent / Highlight | `#E07B39` | School signage board header, building panels |
| Deep Teal | Supporting | `#1A7A6E` | Logo text tones, bus branding |
| Cream / Off-White | Background / Text BG | `#FDF6EC` | Clean backgrounds |
| Pure White | Text / Reverse | `#FFFFFF` | On dark backgrounds |
| Charcoal | Body Text | `#2C2C2C` | All body copy |

> **Primary palette for the website:** Forest Green + White + Amber accent. These reflect the lived visual experience of the school and its brand materials.

### 4.3 Typography

Based on current signage and design direction, the recommended typeface pairing is:

| Role | Typeface | Weight | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Display / Headlines | **Playfair Display** or **Cormorant Garamond** | Bold / SemiBold | Evokes academic tradition and prestige |
| Sub-headings | **Raleway** or **Lato** | SemiBold | Clean and modern |
| Body Copy | **Source Sans Pro** or **Open Sans** | Regular | Highly legible on screen |
| Accent / Quote Text | **Cormorant Garamond Italic** | Italic | For motto, mission quotes |

> All typefaces are available on Google Fonts and free for web use.

## 8. Tone of Voice

CTRS's website and communications should feel:

- **Warm and inviting** — like a trusted institution that genuinely cares about every child
- **Faith-forward but not exclusive** — the Christian foundation is central without being alienating
- **Aspirational but grounded** — academic excellence is real and proven, not boastful
- **Parental and reassuring** — parents are the audience; speak to their hopes and their peace of mind
- **Professional** — not formal or stiff, but clearly a serious, quality institution

**What to avoid:** Overly corporate language, generic phrases like "world-class education," or condescending tone toward parents.

**Sample taglines:**
- "Where Faith Meets Excellence"
- "Building Character. Inspiring Minds."
- "From Crèche to Secondary — A School for Life"
- "Education For God's Glory — and Yours"

## 10. Website Technical Notes

- **Framework recommendation:** Next.js (React) — fast, SEO-friendly, ideal for CMS integration later
- **CMS recommendation:** Sanity.io or Strapi — headless CMS that pairs well with Next.js; easy for non-technical staff to manage content, add images, upload newsletters
- **Hosting recommendation:** Vercel (for Next.js) — free tier available, auto-deploy from GitHub
- **Authentication for Parent Portal:** If the existing portal is a third-party system, the "Parent Portal" button simply links out to that URL. If building new, consider Supabase Auth or Firebase Auth.
- **Image optimisation:** Use Next.js `<Image>` component for automatic WebP conversion and lazy loading. All 79 images are high-resolution JPEGs — they should be optimised before deployment.
- **SEO basics:** Each page should have a unique title tag, meta description, and OG image. The `campus-exterior-building-full-frontage.jpeg` is recommended as the site-wide default OG image.
